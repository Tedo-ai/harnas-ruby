The projection — what will be sent to the provider on the next turn. Mutations applied, streaming deltas collapsed into single messages, turn-bracket meta events (:assistant_turn_started, _completed) hidden.
Reading compactions: when a :compact fires, you'll see a dashed row labeled summary · synthetic · replaces […] — that's the one block the provider actually sees in place of the earlier events. The rows immediately below it (italic, dimmed, tagged replaced by #N) are the original events; they still exist in the Log (see the Timeline tab) but the projection hides them behind the summary.
The raw Log — every Event that entered the Session's Log, in seq order. Every streaming delta is its own row. Mutation events (:compact, :revert) are visible; turn brackets are visible. This is the substrate the Session owns; the context tab is a derived projection over it.
Reading compactions: a :compact row is the mutation event itself — outlined in amber, tagged mutation · hides […]. Events whose seq appears in the compact's replaces list are still in the log (this is an append-only substrate; nothing is deleted), but they're dimmed and tagged replaced by #N to show they've been hidden from downstream projections.
